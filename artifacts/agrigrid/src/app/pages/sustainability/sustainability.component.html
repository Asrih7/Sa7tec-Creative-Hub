The story behind the numbers

By matching irrigation to actual moisture deficit instead of fixed schedules, AgriGrid avoids over-watering. AI-recommended planting windows and drought-resistant rotations cut emissions from machinery and increase yields per drop. Combined with a shared blockchain ledger, every cooperative member earns verifiable sustainability credentials.
